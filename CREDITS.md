## Architecture reference

Dreadmarch, https://github.com/mauricekastelijn/dreadmarch-game , inspected before implementation. Crownmarch follows its ES-module separation of simulation, Three.js view, compatibility view and UI; local dependency bundling; instanced environmental geometry; root game entry point and Pages deployment. Gameplay and procedural medieval assets are new. Dreadmarch's modern Soldier, demon and skeleton GLBs are not included, so their associated model licenses do not apply to this distribution.
